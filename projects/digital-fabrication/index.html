Meet CoolDice

CoolDice explores how a small gesture can set a playful experience in motion. A leftward palm swipe releases a marble into a physical track. When it reaches the end, a digital dice rolls, and a solenoid-operated dispenser releases the corresponding number of candies. Built using Arduino, sensors and digital fabrication, the project connects physical movement, digital feedback and a tangible reward.

One gesture starts the story.

Before starting, the visitor places a marble at START. A leftward swipe of the right palm then sets the sequence in motion.

  1. A visitor places the marble at START
  2. swipes their right palm left
  3. follow the marble
  4. marble run ends
  5. watches the dice roll
  6. collects their candies

03 — The Design Intent: Make the cause feel personal. Make the effect visible.

The "magic" sits in the gap between the hand and the object. The rest of the experience is designed to make that connection readable, then stretch it into anticipation.

  • 01 Distance creates surprise: A touchless gesture separates the action from its physical effect. The intended feeling is direct influence without contact.
  • 02 The marble makes it tangible: The moving ball gives the visitor something real to follow. It carries the sequence forward and makes the wait part of the experience.
  • 03 The dice holds back the answer: The roll adds uncertainty after the physical journey. The visitor can start the experience, but cannot choose its outcome.
  • 04 Candy gives it a physical ending: The number on screen becomes something countable in the hand, returning the digital moment to the physical world.

A physical–digital–physical loop

The interaction combines Arduino, p5.js, sensing, a marble track, an on-screen dice, and a solenoid-operated candy dispenser.

Physical Input: Palm gesture → marble release → track journey. The visitor starts the sequence. The marble provides visible motion.

Digital Decision: Arrival sensor → on-screen dice roll → number. The arrival event triggers the roll. Its result determines the quantity.

Physical Output: Dispensing command → solenoid action → candies. The dispenser translates the digital result into a tangible reward.

Hardware Roles: 01 / SENSING: IR sensing registers physical events. 02 / COORDINATION: Arduino UNO R4 Minima, p5.js coordinates the sequence. 03 / ACTUATION: Motor and solenoid create physical output.

The visitor controls the beginning. Chance controls the ending.
