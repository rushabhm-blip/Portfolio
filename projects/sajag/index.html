SAJAG · Swacch, Surakshit, Sanchar

A city we can care for, together. Project by Rushabh Mahore. Interaction design, System Design, UX Design.

Design Process · Double Diamond Framework

01 Discover, 02 Define, 03 Develop, 04 Deliver. Reimagining How We Care for Our Cities.

Scroll to explore the process

01. Everyday City Experience

See a pothole, wait for a bus, see garbage piling up. When did that become normal? That is where Sajag comes in.

02. The Design Challenge

What: Disconnected transport systems. Why: Information spread across providers. How: Connect mobility, public services, safety, and civic participation.

03. People & Journeys

A city is shared, but lived differently: Student, Women commuter, Street vendor, Delivery worker, Older adult, Shopkeeper, Office worker, Parent.

04. Research Approach & Findings

Primary user research, observation studies, on-ground interviews, contextual inquiry, and synthesized quantitative & qualitative insights.

05. Empathy & Lived Experience

Think, Say, Feel, Do. The Butterfly Effect: small urban incidents lead to far-reaching consequences in public spaces.

06. System Relationships

The Vicious Loops, Iceberg Model (Events, Patterns, System structures, Mental models), Leverage points, and Stakeholder ecosystem.

07. Design Brief

For whom: Residents, commuters and visitors. What: An inclusive companion. To enable: Informed travel choices and safer public spaces. Through: Identifying emerging needs.

08. Idea Exploration

Smart Solutions for Smart City: Safety, Transportation, unsecure area marker, voice comments about space, and application workflow.

09. Patterns into Possibilities & Design Principles

Synthesizing recurring friction patterns into proactive civic-tech touchpoints, guided by core design principles.

10. Experience Strategy · Five Planes of UX

Strategy, Scope, Structure, Skeleton, and Surface planes applied to civic mobility.

11. Information Architecture & Visual Personality

Circular information architecture, tone of voice spectrum, typographic hierarchy, and interface design system.

12. Detailed Journey: Voice-First Assistance

Hands-free navigation, voice reporting, conversational AI for multi-lingual commuters, and accessible emergency triggers.

13. Detailed Journey: Transit, Simplified

End-to-end multi-modal transit planning, live tracking, ticketing, and one pass for all city needs.

14. Detailed Journey: Safety, Simplified

SOS dispatch, live hazard reporting, silent shortcuts, community assistance, and verified routing.

15. Detailed Journey: Parking, Simplified

Real-time parking availability, spot booking, dynamic QR access, and navigation assistance.

16. Detailed Journey: Civic Reporting

Civic reporting: Spot an issue, add evidence, submit report, ward review, assign crew, fix verification, and closed resolution.

17. Detailed Journey: Multilingual Voice & Tone

Inclusive language support across Kannada, Hindi, and English with regional colloquialisms and empathetic cues.

18. Conclusion & Reflection

Synthesizing the complete ecosystem vision: civic accountability, public trust, and resilient city infrastructure.

19. User Testing: From Questions to Design Decisions

User feedback quotes, critical usability findings, and validated product iterations across usability testing cohorts.

20. Learnings & Reflections

Connected services need clear roles, system thinking reveals dependencies, iteration improves clarity, visual consistency supports understanding.
