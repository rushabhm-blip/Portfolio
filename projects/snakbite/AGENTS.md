# Project Guidelines

## Typography Rules
- **All Charts and Data Visualizations**: Must use **Google Sans Flex** (`'Google Sans Flex', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif`).
  - Google Fonts URL: `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Google+Sans+Flex:wght@300;400;500;600;700;800&display=swap`
  - Applies to: titles, subtitles, bar labels, values, axis marks, tooltips, and legends across all present and future chart components.
