Munshiji · Financial Learning & Decision Support Companion

A financial learning & decision support companion. Project by Rushabh Mahore. Interaction design, System Design, UX Design.

Design Process · Double Diamond Framework

01 Discover: Understand financial behavior, 02 Define: Focus the design direction, 03 Develop: Make the experience tangible, 04 Deliver: Test and refine the prototype.

01. Story of Every Month & Behavioral Loops

Salary credited, small amount saved, but where did the money go? Six core loops: Information overload, Comparison trap, Trust confusion, Loss anxiety, Passive investing, Broken habits.

02. Defining the Challenge

Why: Financial platforms prioritize transactions over understanding. What: Beginners struggle with unfamiliar terms and conflicting advice. How: Making learning and saving simple, familiar, and approachable.

03. The Vicious Loops, Iceberg Model & Leverage Points

Identifying vicious reinforcing loops, Iceberg model (Events, Patterns, System structures, Mental models), and Leverage points to interrupt recurring problems.

04. Stakeholder Ecosystem & Munshiji Intervention

Current vs proposed connections across banks, regulators, creators, family, and where Munshiji intervenes to provide verified insights and decision support.

05. Design Brief

For first-time investors, small savers, and uncertain investors: Simple financial lessons, evidence-based claim checks, portfolio insights, and contextual guidance.

06. Ideas & Solutions

Exploration of object-based interactions, Gullak savings, Radio discussions, and tactile learning companions.

07. Design Research Methodology

Discover, Understand, Synthesize, Develop, and Validate phases. Flowchart study of industry benchmarks like Groww.

08. User Interviews & Repeated Patterns

In-depth interviews with participants, uncovering money habits, hesitation, advice sources, and tracking gaps.

09. Persona Creation & Behavioral Dynamics

Detailed personas capturing overwhelmed beginners, FOMO-driven investors, and tradition-oriented savers.

10. Empathy Mapping

Mapping what users Say, Think, Feel, and Do when faced with saving and investing decisions.

11. Patterns & Possibilities

Transforming observed friction points into concrete UX opportunities across learning, trust, clarity, risk, readiness, and progress.

12. Five Planes of User Experience

Strategy, Scope, Structure, Skeleton, and Surface: Defining typography, color foundations, screen states, and accessible navigation.

13. User Flow & Information Architecture

Comprehensive system flowchart linking Home hub, Gullak savings, Learn journeys, Goal tracking, Radio episodes, and Portfolio holdings.

14. Munshiji Identity & Daily Journeys

The warm, patient character identity of Munshiji, alongside the core Home and Gullak coin-drop user journeys.

15. Feature: Small Lessons, Visible Growth

Bite-sized, modular lessons with progress visualizers, interactive practice, and accessible financial concepts.

16. Feature: Make Room for What Matters

Personalized goal setting: Choose a goal, an amount, and a timeline that fits your lifestyle without pressure.

17. Feature: Radio · Money Made Clearer

Audio storytelling and quick takeaways explaining market news, financial history, and investing fundamentals in plain language.

18. Portfolio Insights & Gyroscope Room

Holdings comparison, performance context without jargon, interactive 3D gyroscope exploration of Munshiji's room, and voice queries.
